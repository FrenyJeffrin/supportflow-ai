# SupportFlow Customer Support Policy

SupportFlow customer support is available
Monday through Saturday.

Support agents may assist customers with:

- order tracking
- refunds
- returns
- shipping issues
- warranty questions
- account-related support

AI agents must not claim that an order was refunded,
cancelled, replaced, or modified unless the relevant
backend system confirms that the operation succeeded.

Cases that cannot be resolved automatically should
be escalated by creating a support ticket.
