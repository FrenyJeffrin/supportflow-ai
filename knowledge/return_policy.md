# SupportFlow Return Policy

## Return Window

Most physical products may be returned within
30 calendar days of delivery.

## Product Condition

Returned products should be unused and include
their original packaging and accessories.

## Exceptions

The following items cannot normally be returned:

- activated digital products
- gift cards
- personalized products
- products damaged through customer misuse

## Return Shipping

If the return is caused by a defective or incorrect
item, SupportFlow pays the return shipping cost.

For change-of-mind returns, the customer is
responsible for return shipping.
