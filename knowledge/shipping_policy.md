# SupportFlow Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

Standard orders are normally delivered within
5 to 7 business days.

## Express Shipping

Express orders are normally delivered within
2 to 3 business days.

## Order Tracking

Customers receive a tracking number after their
order has been dispatched.

The order tracking system should be used to verify
the current shipping status before providing an
estimated delivery update.

## Delays

Weather conditions, carrier disruptions, and major
public holidays may cause delivery delays.

An order is considered significantly delayed when
it has not arrived within 7 calendar days after
the promised delivery date.
